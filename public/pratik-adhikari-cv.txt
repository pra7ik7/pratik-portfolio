\section{Research Experience}
\entry{Digital Twin for Real-Time Monitoring \& Short-Term Forecasting
of a Grid-Connected PV System}{2025 -- 2026}

\textit{Final-year project (four-member team), Department of Electrical
Engineering, Pulchowk Campus; Supervisor: Asst. Prof.\ Deependra Neupane}

\begin{itemize}

    \item Built and parameterised a MATLAB/Simulink model of a 335~W
    grid-connected PV system and evaluated it against 41 paired
    measurements collected on site over two days, obtaining 34.76~W
    RMSE (24.1\% nRMSE, $r = 0.918$) when driven by on-site irradiance
    against 114.23~W RMSE ($r = 0.703$) when driven by satellite-derived
    irradiance, quantifying the benefit of site-specific measurement.

    \item Designed and calibrated a low-cost Arduino/LDR irradiance
    sensor against a co-located smartphone-based reference
    (power-law fit, $R^2 = 0.974$, $n = 21$), and used it with
    temperature sensing to drive the model from live serial input,
    demonstrating continuous sensor-to-simulation operation.

    \item Developed a SARIMA$(1,1,1)\times(1,1,1)_{24}$ pipeline for
    day-ahead irradiance and temperature forecasting ($R^2 = 0.921$ and
    $0.964$) and propagated the forecasts through the model, predicting
    next-day PV energy within 0.5\% of the value obtained under measured
    driving conditions.

    \item Analysed irradiance-dependent residuals, attributing
    low-irradiance underprediction to sensor floor limitations and
    high-irradiance overprediction to unmodelled thermal and loss
    effects, and documented both as targets for instrument and model
    refinement.

\end{itemize}

\entry{Deadbeat Predictive Current Control with SVPWM and LCL Filtering
for Grid-Connected Voltage-Source Converters}{2026 -- Present}

\textit{Ongoing research study, Department of Electrical Engineering,
Pulchowk Campus}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Studying deadbeat predictive current control with space-vector
    PWM for a three-phase grid-connected VSC: discrete-time $dq$-frame
    modelling, fixed-frequency 10~kHz modulation, and
    capacitor-current-feedback active damping of an LCL grid interface.

    \item Developing MATLAB/Simulink simulations of a 5~kW, 400~V, 50~Hz
    system comparing L and LCL filtering through resonance,
    harmonic-spectrum, and $P$--$Q$ dynamic-response analyses;
    preliminary results indicate markedly lower current THD for the
    deadbeat-SVPWM with LCL active-damping configuration at matched
    average switching frequency.

    \item Examining LCL resonance, digital-delay effects on active
    damping, and controller de-tuning as practical limits on
    closed-loop stability; the study is currently being extended toward
    a revised control formulation.
\end{itemize}
